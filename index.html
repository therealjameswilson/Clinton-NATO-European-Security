FRUS Production Standard

Compiler Workbench

Document Selection Test

The candidate set should support a thorough, accurate, and reliable record of major U.S. foreign policy decisions and significant diplomatic activity. Keep include, context, exclude, and pending decisions explicit.

Production reference

Chronological Placement

Sort records by Washington time. Place memcons and telcons by the date and time of the conversation, not the later drafting or filing date.

Fields: date, washingtonTime, sortDate, placementNote

First Footnote Readiness

Preserve source, original classification, distribution, drafting information, document identifiers, and whether the President or senior advisers read it.

Fields: sourceNote, classification, distribution, draftingInfo, readBy

Declassification Accounting

Mark full releases, excisions, documents withheld in full, pending review, and the number of omitted lines or pages wherever available.

Fields: declassificationStatus, withheldMaterial
Selection Major decision, significant activity, or supporting context.
Source Repository, collection, case number, NAID, page span, and PDF path.
Editorial Heading, date line, subject line, bracket/excision notes, and annotation.
Access Index terms, people, countries, related documents, and public statements.

Collection Design

What This Page Houses

Released Documents

Direct links to released scans, derivative document-only PDFs, and declassification packets once a source item is verified.

Source Leads

Catalog, FOIA, MDR, and finding-aid leads stay visible even when the underlying conversation or policy paper still needs follow-up review.

Provenance Notes

Source notes track collection names, case numbers, NAIDs, document IDs, source PDF pages, and extraction decisions.

Compiler Filters

Search and lane filters are built in so the record list can scale from a handful of leads to a full document candidate base.

Ingest Checklist

  1. 01 Start with the official FRUS v17 scope statement and status page.
  2. 02 Harvest Clinton Library leader-contact and memcon/telcon leads touching NATO and European security.
  3. 03 Curate State FOIA and Talbott records for NATO enlargement, NATO-Russia, OSCE, Bosnia, and Kosovo context.
  4. 04 Use NARA Scout to identify Clinton NSC European Affairs file units, PRS items, and declassified online candidates.
  5. 05 Normalize dates, participants, source repositories, case numbers, release status, and lane assignment.
  6. 06 Extract only actual document pages when building derivative PDFs, and keep provenance markers in source notes.
  7. 07 Publish new verified leads as the official planned volume moves toward research and compilation.
